# Week 1 slide source and visual notes

Generated conceptual visuals were created on 23 September 2026. They are teaching illustrations, not micrographs, measured evidence, or InPack.AI screenshots. All labels and quantitative annotations are editable PowerPoint elements. The official NCKU and LAiMM logos came from the local course-slide skill assets and appear on each slide without recoloring.

| Slide | Visual asset | Origin and teaching purpose |
|---|---|---|
| H1-01 | package-exploded.png | Image 2.0 conceptual exploded package. Introduces die, package layers, and system connection. Original retained in instructor workspace |
| H1-06 | wafer-dicing.png | Image 2.0 prompt: thin wafer on dicing tape in a frame; vertical narrow saw blade cuts scribe street and stops in tape. Shows singulation geometry. Original retained in instructor workspace |
| H1-07 | die-attach-void.png | Image 2.0 prompt: face-up silicon die, thin continuous attach layer, one small void near edge, organic substrate beneath. Locates an internal interface defect. Original retained in instructor workspace |

| H1-10 | underfill-flow.png | Image 2.0 prompt: one face-down flip-chip die, aligned solder bumps, substrate, and capillary underfill meniscus advancing across the gap. Shows why incomplete flow matters. Original retained in instructor workspace |
