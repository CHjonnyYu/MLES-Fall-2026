MACHINE LEARNING IN ENGINEERING SCIENCE / FALL 2026

One exercise for every class.

16 classroom exercises and 2 optional examination rehearsals. Week 07 has a separate exercise for each of its three one-hour modules. Holidays have no new exercise. These are formative practice, not additional weekly graded submissions.

Each exercise includes a complete worked notebook, detailed instructions, three prediction/experiment/explanation tasks, self-checks, and an evidence export. All use CPU-friendly free tools.
