Change future positions only

Keep positions 0 through 7 fixed. Change positions 8 through 15 using modulo VOCAB, then compare earlier logits. Explain why model.eval() matters.

original_ex=test_x[:1].clone(); changed_ex=original_ex.clone()
changed_ex[:,8:]=(changed_ex[:,8:]+3)%VOCAB
with torch.no_grad():
    original_logits=model(original_ex); changed_logits=model(changed_ex)
delta_ex=float((original_logits[:,:8]-changed_logits[:,:8]).abs().max())
observations['task2']={'unchanged_positions':list(range(8)),'changed_positions':list(range(8,16)),'max_earlier_logit_difference':delta_ex,'passes_1e_minus_6':delta_ex<1e-6}
print(observations['task2'])

Self-check: The earlier logits should agree within the declared tolerance. Token values remain in 0 through 7. This is a behavioral causality check, not a model-accuracy result.

for _ in range(5):
    with torch.no_grad():
        next_ex=model(prefix_ex[:,-CONTEXT:])[:,-1].argmax(dim=-1,keepdim=True)
