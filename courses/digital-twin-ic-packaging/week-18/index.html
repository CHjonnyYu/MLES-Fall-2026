6 January 2027 · Wednesdays, 14:10–17:00

Final examination

The examination has written and oral components. There is no project component. The final counts for 40% of the course grade; homework counts for 30% overall.

Readiness checklist

  1. Explain packaging architecture, process order, and material inputs with units.
  2. Work a thermal or mismatch calculation and state its assumptions.
  3. Distinguish source evidence, model prediction, physical measurement, and uncertainty.
  4. Defend a package choice against a counterexample and identify a next test.

Use the public cases and the relevant weekly handouts to practice. Official examination logistics and any updates are issued through Moodle.
