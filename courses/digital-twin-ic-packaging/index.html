Digital Twin and IC Packaging

Start with the package: its processes, materials and design constraints. Then use digital twins to connect models with measurable engineering evidence.

Learning path

From package to digital twin.

  1. Understand the physical system

    Map packaging processes, compare architectures and reason about material choices before constructing a model.

  2. Connect coupled behavior

    Study how thermal, mechanical and electrical requirements interact in a package and why isolated optimization can fail.

  3. Build an evidence-based twin

    Connect observations and simulation through the InPack.AI workflow. Distinguish model predictions from validated engineering conclusions.

  4. Evaluate design decisions

    Use substrate, interposer, warpage and thermal cases to explain design trade-offs and model limitations.
